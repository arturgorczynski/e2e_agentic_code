## Daily controls

Review open purchase-order lines by supplier, product, ordered quantity and expected date. Subtract linked goods receipts to obtain the outstanding quantity; a late or partial receipt remains visible on the original order. Match every accepted receipt with its warehouse and stock movement before marking a line received. Record the supplier's revised evidence and the review date when the expectation changes.

## Handoffs and exceptions

Send Warehouse Operations the purchase-order line, product, warehouse and outstanding units so it can plan receipts and reservations. Inform Sales and Logistics of a material delay as an inbound risk, with the supplier evidence and next review time; do not convert it into a guaranteed customer date. Escalate an over-receipt, supplier mismatch or receipt without stock movement before closing the order.

## Information handling

Supplier terms and open commitments belong to the purchasing and operations scope. A customer-facing update should state the affected order and confirmed next action without exposing unrelated supplier terms. Keep historic replenishment reconstruction separate from current supplier communications and dates.
