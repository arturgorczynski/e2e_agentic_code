## Daily controls

For every consignment, compare shipment items with the order-line quantities and the linked dispatch stock movements. Record the dispatch date, carrier reference and any separately confirmed delivery date. A partial shipment must show which lines remain unshipped; a dispatched parcel without delivery evidence is still awaiting confirmation. Keep later carrier events in sequence without rewriting the original dispatch record.

## Handoffs and exceptions

Give Support the shipment and carrier references when a customer disputes delivery. Ask Warehouse Operations to investigate a missing dispatch movement or quantity mismatch, and ask Purchasing about an inbound shortage only when it affects a future consignment. Send Sales a revised delivery estimate with its source and uncertainty; an inbound supplier date is not itself a customer promise.

## Information handling

Share a customer's delivery history only with staff working that account or case. State whether each date refers to order acceptance, dispatch or confirmed delivery. Keep the 2011 reconstructed shipment history distinct from current carrier activity when explaining performance.
