## Daily controls

Reconcile each product and warehouse pair from opening stock through goods receipts, dispatches, adjustments and reservations. Match received units to purchase-order lines and goods-receipt records; match dispatched units to shipment lines. Available units are on-hand less reserved units at that warehouse. Investigate a physical-count difference with movement IDs before posting an adjustment or reporting the stock as available.

## Handoffs and exceptions

Give Purchasing the product, warehouse and outstanding inbound quantity when a receipt is late or short. Give Logistics the shipment-line and movement IDs when a pick or dispatch is disputed. Send Support documented condition evidence for a damaged-goods case; a credit decision belongs to Finance. Escalate over-shipment, a movement tied to the wrong warehouse or reserved units above on-hand stock before further picking.

## Information handling

Stock figures are time- and warehouse-specific. Share the dated availability and its source movements with the staff making a fulfillment decision, not as an unconditional future promise. Archival reconstructed movements and current physical stock must remain separate in an operational report.
