## Daily controls

Confirm the customer ID, named contact and account manager before discussing a quote or order. Identify each product by code, check the applicable price observation and distinguish a quotation from an accepted order. For an open order, compare requested units with warehouse availability and the recorded fulfillment status. Record a customer-requested change as an event with its date and owner; do not alter the original basket without a supported business action.

## Handoffs and exceptions

Ask Warehouse Operations and Logistics for line-level dispatch evidence before promising completion or delivery. Send a disputed invoice to Finance with the order and invoice IDs; route damage or service complaints to Support with the customer's own description. When stock is short, state what is available now and what remains uncertain, then agree who will contact the customer next.

## Information handling

Use the selected account scope for customer records, terms and conversation history. A similar trading name is not proof that two accounts are the same. When reviewing long-run sales, separate archival 2011 transactions from current account activity and show the date basis of any comparison.
