## Daily controls

Check each employee's current assignment, effective start, end date, department and location before routing onboarding or a manager request. Record the named sponsor and the work the person needs to perform. Close an expired assignment rather than silently replacing its history. Reconcile the assignment register with the current directory before reporting headcount or asking another team to change access.

## Handoffs and exceptions

Send IT an approved profile request with the employee ID, current assignment and required business scope. Ask the receiving department to confirm a transfer date and owner before the previous assignment is closed. Escalate conflicting or overlapping assignments to the People Operations lead; do not infer an employee's permission solely from a job title or an old location.

## Information handling

Employee contact and assignment records support staffing and access decisions, not broad circulation. Give a business team the current assignment facts needed for its workflow and retain the approval behind an access change. Historical headcount snapshots must not be presented as today's staffing without checking the effective dates.
