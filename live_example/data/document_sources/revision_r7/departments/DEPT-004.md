## Daily controls

Maintain a decision record for each policy change, material exception and cross-team risk. State the evidence available at the decision date, the accountable owner, the options considered and the date for review. Keep the prior policy version accessible when a later decision supersedes it. Distinguish a forecast estimate, a target and an accepted customer commitment in management reporting.

## Handoffs and exceptions

Ask the responsible lead to bring the source order, invoice, shipment, ticket or forecast IDs before approving an exception. Return a disputed figure to Finance or Analytics for reconciliation; return an unconfirmed delivery promise to Logistics and Sales. Record who may communicate the decision and what condition would reopen it. An unresolved issue stays visible with an owner and next review date.

## Information handling

Circulate the minimum detail needed for the decision, with document version and business reference date. Private customer or employee records remain under their normal access scopes even when a summary reaches Leadership. Separate archival 2011 evidence from current operations in board and planning comparisons.
