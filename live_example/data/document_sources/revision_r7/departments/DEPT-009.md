## Daily controls

Open a case with the customer's report in their own terms, the linked account and order IDs, and the time received. Add ticket events for evidence requests, owner changes and confirmed outcomes without editing earlier events. Check shipment, invoice and stock records separately before stating what happened. A case marked in progress is an investigation state, not proof of a refund, delivery or replacement.

## Handoffs and exceptions

Send physical-condition questions to Warehouse Operations with product, shipment and evidence IDs. Ask Logistics to confirm dispatch and delivery events, and Finance to decide a credit or payment adjustment from invoice evidence. Tell the customer which fact is confirmed, which remains disputed, who owns the next action and when it will be reviewed. Escalate an unsupported closure or conflicting event date to the Support lead.

## Information handling

Case notes may contain customer contact details and allegations. Share only the linked records needed by the investigating team, and avoid turning a customer's statement into an established cause. Keep archival support examples and current cases distinct when reporting response times or outcomes.
