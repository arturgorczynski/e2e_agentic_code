## Daily controls

Start a balance review with the invoice ID, posted date, due date and every invoice line. Reconcile allocated payments and credit notes to that invoice; an incoming payment without an allocation does not settle it. Keep the original invoice total, credited amount and outstanding balance as distinct figures. Check the business reference date before describing a balance as overdue, and record the reviewer and evidence IDs used.

## Handoffs and exceptions

Send a damaged-goods or return question to Support with the invoice and order IDs, and ask Warehouse Operations for physical receipt evidence before treating stock as returned. A credit note records an adjustment; it does not prove a cash refund. Escalate an unmatched receipt, duplicate allocation or negative balance to the Finance owner before issuing a statement or collection request.

## Information handling

Share account balances only within the selected commercial and finance scope. A report export remains owned by its creator until a separate distribution decision is recorded. Label archival invoices and current invoices with their respective posting dates; do not merge their balances into a present-day account statement.
