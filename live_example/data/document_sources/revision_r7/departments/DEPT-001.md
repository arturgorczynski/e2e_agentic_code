## Daily controls

For each forecast run, record the product, target month, method, lookback, training cutoff and input fingerprint. Reconcile billed invoice lines within that cutoff before comparing the run with a sales target. After the target month closes, calculate the error against billed units and state whether actuals are complete. Keep the earlier run when assumptions change; issue a new run with a new identifier instead of overwriting the result.

## Handoffs and exceptions

Give Sales the cohort definition, the forecast and target as separate numbers, and the uncertainty behind any proposed explanation. Ask Finance to resolve missing or disputed invoice lines before publishing a sales movement. Send Purchasing a product-level demand change only with the cutoff and units that support it. Log the owner, decision date and next review when a material variance remains unexplained.

## Information handling

Publish aggregate figures with period boundaries, GBP units and source references. Keep archival 2011 transactions and current account activity in separate dated cohorts before any combined comparison. A customer's sales history or an employee assignment is supporting data for an authorized review, not a general distribution list.
