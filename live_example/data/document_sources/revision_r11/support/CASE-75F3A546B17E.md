## Applicable return window

`POL-RETURNS-001:v2` was the version effective on the 24 August delivery
date. It provided a 30-calendar-day ordinary request window. The request
arrived after two days, so it was within that window for **review**. The
current v3 policy began on 20 October and does not retroactively decide this
August request. Being within the window is not acceptance of a return, a
warehouse receipt, a credit note or a cash refund.

## Recorded activity and current decision

The ticket opened on 26 August with an evidence request. The 21 October case
review recorded the delivery and policy-version check, identified the two
possible one-unit lines, and asked for the affected line ID, quantity and
condition evidence. The ticket is **in progress** while those details and a
Warehouse Operations assessment remain outstanding. No acceptance or physical
return is established by the case record.

Invoice `SCN-BILL-75F3A546B17E-02` posted GBP 124.00. At this review it has
no linked payment allocation or credit note. This is an invoice observation,
not a decision about compensation. Finance would separately review any later
credit; Warehouse Operations would separately record receipt and condition
if goods came back. Support must not promise either outcome from this window
calculation alone.

quantity and condition evidence. Warehouse Operations owns any physical
inspection. Finance owns any credit decision against the posted invoice.
Retain the original 26 August report, the 21 October review event, the
