# Damaged-goods complaint handling

## Purpose and ownership

Customer Support owns the case record and coordinates the investigation. The
warehouse checks packing and stock evidence; Logistics checks dispatch and
carrier evidence; Finance decides whether any financial adjustment is
appropriate. The complaint remains an account of what the customer reported
until those records have been reviewed.

## Intake

Open or locate the service ticket and confirm its customer, order and affected
consignment. Record the product code, quantity, nature of the reported damage,
date first noticed and any photographs or packaging information supplied by
the customer. Acknowledge the report without assigning fault. If the order or
shipment reference is missing, request it before drawing conclusions from a
similar-looking order.

## Investigation and status

Compare the ordered and dispatched quantities at order-line level. Review the
dispatch date, carrier reference and recorded delivery date separately. Where
the claim concerns the condition of goods, ask the warehouse to inspect any
returned units and retain the inspection result. Note what is confirmed, what
remains a customer statement and what evidence is still missing.

An **open** ticket becomes **in_progress** only after the assigned employee
begins a documented review and the permitted status change is approved. Record
the acting employee, ticket ID, previous and new status, reason and operation
reference. Repeating the same approved operation must not create another
transition. Mark the case **resolved** only when the evidence and actual outcome
are recorded in a dated ticket event. Keep the original description and all
earlier events available.

## Resolution and escalation

Support explains the reviewed outcome to the customer and identifies any
remaining action owner. A proposed replacement, return, credit or refund is a
separate decision; the complaint status alone authorizes none of them. Escalate
quantity differences to Operations and financial remedies to Finance with the
relevant order, shipment, ticket and invoice IDs. If the status proposal is
rejected, retain the existing ticket state and record no completed change.
