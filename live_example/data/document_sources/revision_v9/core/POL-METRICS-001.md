# Sales measures and comparable periods

## Purpose and ownership

The Analytics team publishes comparable sales measures for account and product
reviews. Every result must state its currency, date range, population and
accounting basis. Acme's posted sales measure is the sum of invoice line amounts
less related credit notes attributed to the original invoice cohort. It is not
cash received, gross margin, tax, or audited profit.

## Period selection

Use complete calendar periods when describing a month-on-month change. The
source-history reference date is 9 December 2011, so December 2011 is partial.
Contemporary fictional trading records have a separate reference date and must
be labelled as simulated when included. Never combine a historical source
cohort with a simulated cohort without showing both components and the combined
scope explicitly. Payment timing does not change the month to which an invoice
belongs.

## Calculation

For a like-for-like comparison, select the same customer and product scope in
both periods. The absolute movement is current-period value minus prior-period
value. Divide that movement by the prior-period value for the percentage
movement. If the prior value is zero, give the absolute movement and state that
the percentage is undefined. Show the period boundaries, pence total and GBP
display value so a reviewer can reproduce the arithmetic.

## Interpretation and evidence

A numerical change does not establish its cause. Identify proposed explanations
as hypotheses and name the additional account, stock, pricing or service
evidence needed to assess them. Cite the prepared aggregate or contributing
invoice and credit-note IDs. Retain the dataset version and calculation date in
any saved report. Excluded source baskets remain outside the selected baseline
population; their omission must be disclosed when a result is presented as a
measure of that source population.
