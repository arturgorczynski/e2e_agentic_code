# Comparable sales metrics

State the unit, period, customer/product scope and accounting basis before interpreting
a change. Acme's posted sales view sums line-rounded invoice amounts less related credit
notes. It is a learning metric, not audited profit, cash receipts or a tax calculation.
Payment timing must not change which month an invoice belongs to.
Related credits are attributed to the original invoice cohort as of the reference
date. This is not a ledger of credit-note postings by their issue month.

## Comparisons

Use matching complete periods. October and November 2011 are complete in the selected
history; December ends on the fixed business reference date. A partial December total
must not be compared with an entire prior month without an explicit qualification.
The absolute change is current minus previous; the percentage change divides that
difference by the previous value. When the prior value is zero, report the absolute
change and label percentage change undefined.

## Evidence and uncertainty

A decline establishes a difference, not its cause. Do not invent churn, pricing pressure,
supplier failure or seasonality as explanations merely because they sound plausible.
Separate an observed fact from a hypothesis and identify the additional evidence needed
to test the hypothesis. Cite the contributing invoice scope or prepared aggregate.

The baseline selects complete eligible public baskets. Excluded source baskets remain
in the staging files and may change a broader population's totals. Synthetic DEMO records
have their own customer IDs and must not be described as measured UCI customer behavior.
Record the dataset version in saved reports so later results can be compared correctly.
